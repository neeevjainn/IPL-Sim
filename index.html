How It Works

Everything you need to know about the simulation.

🔄 Season Flow

  1. Admin sets up teams and imports players
  2. Auction: lots drawn, players assigned, budgets tracked
  3. Each matchday: Admin sets XI + aggression for all teams → simulate → results
  4. Delegates review analytics PDF to advise the chair on strategy
  5. Trade windows open every 2 matchdays
  6. Top 4 enter Playoffs → Champion revealed

🎚️ Aggression System

  • 20–39 Cautious: Safer batting, fewer wickets but slower scoring.
  • 40–59 Calculated: Slightly conservative. Good for tricky pitches.
  • 60 Balanced: Default. No modifiers.
  • 61–79 Attacking: More boundaries, some wicket risk.
  • 80–100 Ultra: Max sixes, max wickets — go big or go home.

🌱 Pitch Types

  • Batting Paradise: High boundaries, bat first.
  • Spin Friendly: Spinners get bonus wickets — pick 2+.
  • Pace Heaven: Pacers get swing bonus early.
  • Balanced: No strong advantage.

🤕 Injuries

  • 1-2 injuries every 3-4 matchdays across all teams.
  • Each team has 1 injury-prone player (marked ⚠️ in reports).
  • Season Crisis at 3/4 mark: a random player is season-ended.
  • Injured players are shown in Matchday → Injury Report.

📥 Analytics & Reports

  • Go to Reports tab to download the full analytics HTML file.
  • Print or share it with delegates before strategy sessions.
  • It includes all squad ratings, injury status, pitch guide, and season stats.
  • Delegates advise the chair verbally — chair enters XI and aggression.

⚡ Super Over

  • Triggered automatically when scores are tied.
  • Top 2 batters + 1 bowler per side. 1 over each.
  • If still tied: team with more sixes in Super Over wins.
