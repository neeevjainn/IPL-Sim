How It Works

Everything delegates and chair need to know about the simulation.

🔄 Season Flow

  1. Admin sets up teams and imports players
  2. Auction: lots drawn, players assigned, budgets tracked
  3. Each matchday: delegates set XI + aggression → Admin simulates → Results show
  4. Trade windows open every 2 matchdays
  5. Top 4 enter Playoffs → Qualifier 1, Eliminator, Qualifier 2, Final
  6. Season champion revealed with ceremony animation

👥 Playing XI Rules

  • Each delegate selects exactly 11 players from their squad before the matchday locks.
  • Injured players are greyed out and cannot be selected.
  • Use Auto-Pick for the best available 11 by combined rating.
  • Admin can override any team's XI before simulation starts.
  • Batting order follows the order you selected players in.

🎚️ Aggression System

  • 20–39 (Cautious): Safer batting, fewer wickets but slower scoring.
  • 40–59 (Calculated): Slightly conservative. Good for tricky pitches.
  • 60 (Balanced): Default. No modifiers applied.
  • 61–79 (Attacking): More boundaries, some extra wicket risk.
  • 80–100 (Ultra): High six probability, high wicket probability — high risk, high reward.

🌱 Pitch Types

  • Batting Paradise: Wankhede, Chinnaswamy, Eden Gardens — high boundary probability, fewer wickets.
  • Spin Friendly: Chepauk, Rajiv Gandhi, Sawai — spinners get bonus wickets, slightly lower run rate.
  • Pace Heaven: Mohali, Dharamsala, Ekana — pacers get swing bonus, early boundaries.
  • Balanced: Narendra Modi, Arun Jaitley — standard probabilities.

⚡ Powerplay (Overs 1–6)

  • Boundary probability ×1.20 during overs 1–6.
  • Singles probability ×0.75 — batters go for it early.
  • Live card shows an orange POWERPLAY badge during these overs.
  • PP stats shown separately in each scorecard.

🤕 Injuries

  • Standard: 6% chance per active player after each matchday. Miss 1 MD.
  • Crisis Event: At matchday ¾ of the season, the top team's best player is season-ended. Triggers a dramatic red animation.
  • Injured players are auto-flagged in the XI selector — pick a substitute!

⚡🏏 Super Over

  • If both teams finish on the same score, a Super Over is triggered automatically.
  • Top 2 batters + 1 bowler per side. 1 over each.
  • If still tied: team with more sixes in the Super Over wins.
  • Full Super Over scorecard available after the match.

🔄 Trade Desk

  • Trade windows open every 2 matchdays.
  • Only the admin/chair can execute trades — negotiate in the room!
  • All trades are logged and visible in Scorecards and Stats.
